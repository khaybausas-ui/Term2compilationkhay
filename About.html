ABOUT NASUGBU FIESTA

The Fiesta De Los Toros (also known as the Suwagan Festival) is an annual community celebration held every December 2 and 3 in Nasugbu, Batangas, honoring the town's patron saint, St. Francis Xavier.

Core Tradition & Origin

Patron Saint & Heritage: Started in 2006, the festival pays tribute to St. Francis Xavier with religious devotions, including dawn masses at St. Francis Xavier Parish Church.

Cow Blessing & Symbolism: A central tradition is the blessing of male and female cows to pray for prosperous livestock and high-quality Batangas beef.

Key Events & Schedule

Day 1 (December 2)

Religious & Parade Events: Features dawn masses and the colorful Bailar Ala Toro Festival Dance Parade.

Entertainment & Activities: Includes dance exhibitions at Plaza De Roxas, TikTok and Videoke challenges, live music, and Takbo De Los Toros fun run.

Day 2 (December 3)

Parade Highlights: Features the grand Parada de los Toros with decorated cows, carromatas, and marching bands.

Cultural Traditions: Highlights include the playful Suwagan ng Toro paper-mache bull chase, Bayle, and Sayaw ng Papuri, concluding with fireworks and concert.
